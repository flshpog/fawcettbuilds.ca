06 / Questions

The things people ask first.

Do I need a permit?

Most electrical work in Ontario needs a notification filed with the Electrical Safety Authority, and structural work usually needs a building permit from your municipality. We handle the filing and the inspection booking as part of the job. You should never be the one chasing an inspector.

What does an estimate cost?

Nothing. We come out, look at the job, and send a written scope and price. If the job turns out to need a design or engineering drawing before it can be priced properly, we will tell you that up front rather than guessing at a number.

Fixed price or hourly?

Defined jobs get a fixed price, so surprises are our problem and not yours. Open-ended work, such as troubleshooting a fault or opening up a wall that nobody has seen inside of, is hourly, quoted with a not-to-exceed number wherever we can.

How long until you can start?

It depends on the season and the size of the job. Small electrical calls usually land within a week or two; a renovation or addition gets scheduled further out. We will give you a real date rather than a hopeful one.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Master electrician licensing through the Electrical Safety Authority, plus liability insurance and WSIB coverage. Ask and we will send the certificates before you commit to anything. Any contractor who will not do that is telling you something.

Do you take on small jobs?

Yes. A dead outlet, a ceiling fan, a sagging deck board. Small jobs are how most of our bigger ones started.
